FAQ

Questions people ask first

What do I pay?

Developers pay a success fee only when capital is raised: 1.5% on the first €3M and 1% on any amount above that. For example, raising €5M costs €65,000. There is no retainer and no upfront fee. The terms are written down and agreed before any work begins.

What does the AI agent actually do?

It builds your profile from what you tell it, finds the gaps investors will ask about, matches you with lenders and investors whose criteria fit, drafts the documents and messages, and follows up. It works around the clock for you.

What stays with people?

Every decision that carries risk. You approve what the project says and who sees it, and you confirm every send. Our team reviews materials, negotiates terms and coordinates legal work. Nothing is sent on your behalf without your approval.

Where do the investors come from?

From our network of lenders, funds, family offices and private investors active in Spain and Portugal. The agent keeps their criteria current. Investors are qualified before they see project details and opt in to every introduction.

I am an investor. What do I get?

An agent that reads every project we receive, checks it against your mandate and brings you only the matches, with the numbers already structured. You decide what to review and who to talk to.

Which projects are a fit?

New development, conversion and repositioning in Spain and Portugal, from roughly €500k to several million euros of capital. Permits and sponsor equity improve the options at every stage.

Are you a financial adviser?

No. Cobuildy S.L. acts as an introducer. We do not give investment advice and we do not hold client funds. Investors make their own decisions with their own advisers.
